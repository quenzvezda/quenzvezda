## Hi, I'm Rama Almer Felix 👋

**Backend Engineer** with 5+ years of experience building enterprise applications, distributed systems, and backend integrations. I work mostly with **C#/.NET** and **Java**, and I care about software that is maintainable, reliable, and built to scale.

Currently a **Senior Software Engineer at PT. Bank Negara Indonesia (BNI)**, where I work on an internal Audit Management System built on Domain-Driven Design and Kafka-based messaging.

### What I Work On
- 🏗️ Backend architecture with **Domain-Driven Design** and **Clean Architecture**
- 🔄 Event-driven systems and asynchronous processing with **Kafka**, worker services, and job queues
- 📊 ERP domains: accounting, procurement, inventory, and accounts payable workflows
- 🚀 Delivery from domain modelling through testing, CI/CD, deployment, and production support
